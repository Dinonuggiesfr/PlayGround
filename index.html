HOW TO PLAY

• Shields (blue) regen after 4s without damage. HP (red) regens slower — grab Light Orbs (cyan) dropped by majors to heal.
• Grenade [E]: solar vortex, big DoT. Melee [F]: knife lunge when close. Rift [C]: healing circle.
• Super [X]: class ultimate at 100%. Kills feed charge (+6%), precision kills +10%.
• Weapons: 1 auto-rifle (adds), 2 hand-cannon (precision ×2 headshot), 3 rocket (splash + boss DPS, watch the blast!).
• Waves: each wave spawns Thralls (rush), Acolytes (shoot), Knights (tanky). Wave 5 & 10 = WARDEN boss.
• Glimmer ✦ = score currency. Spend nothing — flex it.
